# T4 — Ergonomics (gorilla-arm) + Embodiment/Installation Case Studies

## Focus
(a) Physical fatigue of held/raised-hand mid-air gestures and how to mitigate it.
(b) How real embodiment/mirror installations (Kinect, AR mirrors, avatar puppeteering)
structured their gesture vocabulary — what worked in the wild.

## Sub-questions
1. **Gorilla-arm fatigue:** definition, causes; "Consumed Endurance" metric (Hincapié-Ramos
   et al., CHI 2014); how arm elevation/duration drive fatigue; recommended interaction
   height/plane; keep hands low, short holds. Concrete guidance/thresholds.
2. **Ergonomic mitigations:** lower interaction plane, elbow-supported/resting-arm input,
   brief dwell vs long hold, avoiding sustained above-shoulder poses.
3. **Embodiment / full-body puppeteering installations:** how systems that map the user's
   body to an avatar handle MENU/selection when the body is the controller — reserved poses,
   T-pose reset, timeouts, secondary modality. Examples: Kinect avatar mirrors, "Reflections,"
   museum AR mirrors, theme-park/character embodiment, virtual-production puppeteering.
4. **Case-study gesture vocabularies:** what selection/confirm gestures shipped in notable
   public gesture installations and why; failure modes reported.

## Evidence to find
- "Consumed Endurance" gorilla-arm paper (abstract/HTML/summary), follow-ups.
- Ultraleap/Microsoft ergonomics guidance; visionOS comfort guidance.
- Museum/experiential AR-mirror & Kinect-installation postmortems, case studies, talks.
